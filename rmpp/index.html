rmpp-hwr-db · writers wanted

Teach the tablet to read your handwriting, in your language.

Handwriting recognition is only as good as the handwriting it has seen. rmpp-hwr-db is a new, open dataset of real pen strokes written on the Paper Pro, by the people who use it. It is just starting: the first writers shape it the most.

  • Write on your tabletPractice pages land in a notebook. Write the lines, and your strokes are shared without your name.
  • Every languageKorean and English are open now. Ask for yours and it opens once its first sentences are in.
  • Open for everyoneCC BY 4.0: pen strokes with timing, free for any recognizer, research or app.
  • Better text, for youIt trains rmpp-hwr, so “convert to text” gets better at your script.

What's inside

Each one is optional. None needs xovi, and they keep working after a reboot.

rmpp-lang

On-screen and hardware keyboards and fonts per language: Korean (with Old Hangul), Japanese, Chinese, Russian.

rmpp-hwr

Handwriting to text, on the tablet: lasso a few lines or convert a page. Korean, English, Chinese, Japanese.

rmpp-bt

A Bluetooth page in Settings: search, pair, connect, forget. Keyboards work like the Type Folio.

rmpp-tailscale

Tailscale with a Settings page: on/off, log in by QR code, devices, SSH to the tablet from anywhere.

rmpp-refresh

One button in the notebook toolbar that redraws the whole screen and clears ghosting.

rmpp-hwr-dbCC BY 4.0

An open handwriting dataset written on the Paper Pro. Add your own handwriting in your language.

rmpp-studio

Record, edit and export tablet tutorials and screenshots from your PC; drive the tablet by script.

Your language isn't on the keyboard yet?

rmpp-lang adds keyboards and fonts one language at a time. Ask for yours with an issue, or add it yourself with a pull request: the source is free to use. Every new language must be tested on a real Paper Pro, with screenshots made with rmpp-studio.

Getting started

About 15 minutes the first time. You need a computer (Windows, macOS or Linux) and the tablet's USB-C cable.

  1. Turn on developer mode

    Settings › General › Paper Tablet › Software › Advanced › Developer Mode.

    This factory-resets the tablet. Sync your notebooks to your reMarkable account first; they come back after you sign in again. See reMarkable's developer mode guide.

  2. Find the root password

    Settings › General › Help › About › Copyrights and licenses. The SSH password is under “General information”. The setup app asks for it once, then uses a key.

  3. Connect the tablet

    Plug in the USB-C cable. The tablet appears at 10.11.99.1. (Wi-Fi works too, once SSH over Wi-Fi is turned on: ssh root@10.11.99.1 rm-ssh-over-wlan on.)

  4. Run rmpp setup

    Download the app for your system, connect, tick what you want, and press Install. The tablet's screen restarts once or twice.

    The app is not signed: Windows shows “Windows protected your PC” (More info › Run anyway), and macOS asks you to allow it in System Settings › Privacy & Security. Prefer a terminal? Use the install scripts.

  5. After a reMarkable OS update

    Open rmpp setup again and press Restore. An OS update replaces the system files where the add-ons live; your settings and notebooks are kept.
